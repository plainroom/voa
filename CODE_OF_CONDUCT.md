# Code of Conduct

Be decent to the people who use voa and the people who work on it.

- Assume good faith. Critique the change, not the person.
- No harassment, slurs, or sexual attention that was not asked for.
- Never post tokens, passwords, or someone else's mail. That includes issues and pull requests.

To report a problem with someone's conduct, contact the maintainer through their GitHub profile (https://github.com/pseudonym-dev). Vulnerability reports stay on the path in SECURITY.md.
